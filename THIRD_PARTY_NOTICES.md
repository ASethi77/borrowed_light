# Portable thumbnail codecs

The bundled WebAssembly thumbnail module includes libjpeg-turbo 3.2.0, libpng 1.6.59,
and zlib 1.3.2. Exact source commits and archive digests are recorded in
`codec/sources.json`. Their upstream license notices are distributed in
`dist/codec/licenses/` in the extension. The JavaScript loader is generated by
Emscripten 4.0.20 (MIT/University of Illinois-NCSA licensed).
